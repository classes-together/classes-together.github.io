Elevator Pitch

Classes Together creates a convenient space for informal academic discussion and streamline invitations. There are not many easy or convenient ways for students to communicate informally with their classmates outside of class. Additionally, invitations from other informal platforms are time-consuming to send and do not always stay open. Classes Together automatically enrolls students in forums for their current classes, which stay open to join for the full class duration.

Problem Description

Learning management systems (LMS) are for formal academic discussion and discourage students from asking questions due to their strict atmosphere (Lav et al., 2025). Informal instant messaging platforms provide a relaxed atmosphere, but they are not originally intended for academics or come with courses built in.

LMS only allow instructors or administrators to customize courses. While instant messengers allow user customization, popular options do not have built-in academic features.

Only LMS instructors or administrators may create invites. And while users on instant messengers may, invititations must be manually sent out and typically expire, causing students to miss invitations.

Customer and End User

Customer: Colleges/Universities

End-User: College/University students

Solution Statement

Classes Together will eliminate the time lost by manually sending out invitations and reentry of course and academic information into a new profile. It will also provide an informal setting that is still academic oriented.
